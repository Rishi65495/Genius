Deep Work / Chapter 4 / The Rules

Quit Social Media

High-quality work requires sustained attention. The ability to concentrate without distraction is becoming increasingly valuable in a world where interruptions are constant.

Clarity about what deserves your attention is the first step toward meaningful progress.

Instead of allowing every notification to define your next action, create a deliberate environment. Choose a small window of time, remove the obvious distractions, and begin with a clear intention.

Reading is not a race. It is a conversation with an idea. Let the idea finish its sentence before you reach for something else.

The goal is not to avoid all modern tools. The goal is to make sure your tools serve your values instead of quietly replacing them.
